This Agreement is made on June 10, 2026 between Harbor & Vine Restaurant Group ("Client") and Esi Boateng, trading as Boateng Digital ("Contractor").

1. SERVICES. Contractor will design and build a responsive website with online table reservations for Client, as described in Schedule A (the "Services").

2. FEES AND PAYMENT. Client shall pay a fixed fee of USD 6,000: 40% on signing, 30% on delivery of the staging site, and 30% on launch. Invoices are due within fourteen (14) days. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3. TIMELINE. Contractor will deliver the staging site by August 1, 2026 and launch the website by August 29, 2026, provided Client supplies all content by July 10, 2026.

4. CHANGES. Work outside Schedule A will be quoted separately and billed at USD 50 per hour.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. On receipt of full payment, Contractor assigns to Client all rights in the final website design and content created for Client. Contractor keeps ownership of its pre-existing code libraries and grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use them as part of the website.

6. PORTFOLIO. Contractor may display the website in its portfolio after launch.

7. WARRANTY. For sixty (60) days after launch, Contractor will fix any defects in the Services at no charge.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Contractor's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid by Client. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

9. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement with ten (10) business days' written notice. On termination, Client pays for all work completed to date, and the signing payment is non-refundable.

10. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR. Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee of Client.

11. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario, Canada.
