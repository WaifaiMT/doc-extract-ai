COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

(Fictional sample for testing. Not a real agreement.)

This Lease is made on April 1, 2026 between Adjei Properties Limited ("Landlord") and Kumasi Fresh Juices Ltd. ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant the ground-floor shop at Plot 14, Adum Road, Kumasi (the "Premises").

2. TERM. The lease runs for three (3) years, starting May 1, 2026 and ending April 30, 2029.

3. RENT. Tenant shall pay rent of GHS 4,500 per month, payable in advance on the first day of each month. Rent increases by 8% at the start of each lease year.

4. ADVANCE RENT AND DEPOSIT. On signing, Tenant shall pay twelve (12) months' rent in advance and a security deposit of GHS 9,000. Landlord will return the deposit within thirty (30) days after the lease ends, less the cost of repairing any damage beyond normal wear and tear.

5. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL. This Lease renews automatically for successive three-year terms unless Tenant gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred and eighty (180) days before the end of the current term.

6. USE. Tenant shall use the Premises only to prepare and sell juices and light food.

7. MAINTENANCE. Tenant is responsible for all repairs to the interior of the Premises, including plumbing and electrical fittings. Landlord is responsible for the roof and the external structure.

8. INSURANCE. Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of at least GHS 200,000.

9. INDEMNITY. Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against all claims, losses, and costs arising from Tenant's use of the Premises, without limit.

10. EARLY TERMINATION. Tenant may not terminate this Lease before the end of the term. If Tenant leaves early, all rent for the remainder of the term becomes due immediately.

11. GOVERNING LAW. This Lease is governed by the laws of Ghana.

Landlord: Kwabena Adjei, Director, Adjei Properties Limited
Tenant: Abena Sarpong, Director, Kumasi Fresh Juices Ltd.
